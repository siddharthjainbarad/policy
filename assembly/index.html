Privacy Policy — Assembly Language

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Assembly Language Guide (“the App”, package com.androfrenzy.assembly), made by AndroFrenzy (“we”, “us”), handles information. Our other apps are covered by the AndroFrenzy privacy policy.

Overview

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, and we have no servers that receive it. The App has no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your study progress stays on your device. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show them, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Chapters you have read, your reading position, favourites, streak and daily goal.
  • Quiz scores, flashcard progress, text size, reading tone, theme and reminder time.
  • How many free quiz questions and flashcards you have used and any you unlocked by watching an ad.

This data is stored only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. We cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows full-screen ads between chapters and after some quizzes, and you can choose to watch a short video ad to unlock more quiz questions and flashcards or 30 minutes without ads. Video ads play only when you tap to watch one. Your study progress and app content are never passed to the ad provider.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions with adsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (for example crash and performance data from the ad SDK)Analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google’s policy for partner apps. You can turn off personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.

Quiz questions

Each time the App starts, it downloads the latest quiz questions: a public file hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any web request, this lets GitHub see your IP address. Nothing else is sent: no identifiers, no answers and nothing about your progress. A copy is kept on your device so quizzes also work offline.

Interactive exercises

Animations, simulators, calculators and games inside chapters run entirely on your device. Anything you type or choose in them stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

Daily study reminders are scheduled on your phone by the App itself; no server is involved. The App asks for notification permission and restarts your reminder after the phone reboots. The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data.

Ratings and sharing

“Rate this app” and the occasional rating prompt use Google Play’s in-app review, handled by Google Play. When you share the App or a chapter, the App hands a link and text to the app you pick (such as WhatsApp). We do not see what you share.

Children’s privacy

The App is a study aid for students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Clear the App’s data or uninstall it to delete everything it stored on your device.
  • Manage personalised ads and your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Ad data is held by Google under the policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
