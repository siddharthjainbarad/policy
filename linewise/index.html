LineWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how LineWise - Dual SIM Messaging (“LineWise”, the “App”), an Android text messaging app made by AndroFrenzy (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to LineWise. Other AndroFrenzy apps are covered by their own policy.

The short version

Your texts, contacts and SIM details are handled only on your phone. LineWise has no servers and no accounts, and we never receive, sell or share your messages.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data (described below). Your messages are never used for ads.

What LineWise accesses and why

  • Text messages (SMS and MMS): to show, send, sort, search, back up and delete your texts. LineWise only reads them while it is your default SMS app.
  • Contacts (optional): to show names and photos instead of numbers, and to find people when you start a new message.
  • SIM and phone number details (optional): to label each SIM line and send every text from the SIM you chose.
  • Notifications (optional): to alert you to new texts and show which SIM they arrived on.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock (optional): only if you turn on App lock. LineWise never sees your fingerprint; Android checks it.

Where your data goes

Messages, contacts, SIM details and settings stay on your phone. LineWise uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases. Texts you send go through your mobile carrier like any SMS.

Features that read your texts, such as one-time code detection and the Money summary of bank texts, run entirely on your phone. LineWise never connects to your bank.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Without LineWise Premium, the chat list shows one small ad, clearly labelled “Ad”. You can also choose to watch a short video ad to try a Premium feature; these play only when you tap to watch one. Ads never appear inside chats, search or notifications, and message, contact or phone number data is never passed to the ad provider.

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google’s policy for partner apps. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before ads load, and you can change your choice in LineWise under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.

Purchases

LineWise Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. LineWise only learns whether you own it; it never sees your payment details. Payments are covered by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Backups and exports you choose

If you export a chat, back up your messages or turn on weekly backups, LineWise writes a file only to the place you pick (for example Files or Google Drive). That file is then controlled by you and the service you chose.

Automatic features

Code, money, bill and parcel detection run on your phone. Auto-reply only sends the message you wrote, only on the SIM you turned it on for, at most once every 12 hours per person, and never to short codes or businesses.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Uninstalling LineWise removes its settings from your phone.
  • Your texts stay in Android’s message store, where any SMS app you choose can see them. While LineWise is your default SMS app you can delete texts in it.
  • We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Ad data is held by Google under the policy linked above.

Security

Your messages never leave your phone through LineWise, apart from texts you send through your carrier and backups or exports you start. Ad and purchase traffic is encrypted by Google’s SDKs. App lock adds a fingerprint or screen-lock check before LineWise opens.

Children

LineWise is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and shown in the App under Settings → Privacy policy, with a new “Last updated” date.
